Privacy notice

  • What we store: your name, email, the documents you upload (Aadhaar, marksheets, photo, signature) and the details read from them.
  • Why: only to fill your exam application form for you. We do not sell or share your data.
  • Who can see it: you, and the Exam Buddy staff who fill your form. Staff see your Aadhaar number masked (XXXX XXXX 1234) and every time they open your documents is written to a log.
  • How long: your documents and details are deleted automatically 30 days after your application is marked Completed.
  • Your control: you can delete all your documents and details at any time from My profile, using "Delete my data".
  • Your responsibility: check every detail before confirming. Exam Buddy is not an official exam portal. OTP, CAPTCHA, payment and final submission are always done with you.
